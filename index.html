    How it’s made

    Many days, stacked into one map

    1. Daily satellite imagesMar 15 – Jun 30, the full planting window.
    2. Water checked each dayEvery 3 m pixel is labelled water or dry.
    3. Days combinedEach pixel keeps its share of usable days under water.
    4. One percentage layer0–100% per pixel — view it at 70, 80 or 90% on the download map.
