Terms of use

You accept these terms once, when you first sign in. We record the date and the version you accepted.

This layer is not a prevented-planting eligibility determination. It is evidence about field conditions. Eligibility decisions remain with RMA and the procedures that govern them.

Permitted use

  • Crop-insurance work by Approved Insurance Providers and RMA offices.
  • Research, teaching and public-interest analysis.
  • Combining the layer with your own GIS data inside your organisation.

Citation and attribution

Any product, report or map that uses the layer must credit both organisations:

Planting Season Water Persistence layer, v1.0-beta (2025 service year).
Funded by the USDA Risk Management Agency. Produced by the
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Accessed [date] from
https://waterhub.illinois.edu

Redistribution

  • Share derived maps and summaries freely, with the citation above.
  • Do not republish the raw rasters. Point others to this hub so they get current versions.
  • Do not join the rasters to producer, policy, parcel or field-boundary records for redistribution.

No warranty

The layer is provided “as is”. It is derived from satellite imagery and has a measured error rate (see accuracy). Neither UIUC nor USDA RMA warrants its accuracy for any single field, date or purpose.

Your account

Accounts are personal. Don’t share credentials. We record downloads (who, when, and what selection) to report usage to RMA. We use no advertising or third-party trackers.

Changes to these terms

If the terms change, the new version is posted in Version updates and you’ll be asked to accept it at your next sign-in.
