About the hub

One place to get the Planting Season Water Persistence layer, its documentation and its update history — replacing files shared one by one.

Prairie Pothole counties in North Dakota and South Dakota see standing water come and go through the planting season, and that water is hard to document consistently from the ground. This hub distributes a satellite-derived record of where water stood and for how long, so Approved Insurance Providers and RMA offices can bring the same evidence into their own review — self-service, versioned, and without contacting the research team for every file. It replaces ad-hoc file sharing with one place to get the current data, its documentation and its update history. Questions or problem reports go to the UIUC project team, below.
