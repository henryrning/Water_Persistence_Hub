Cautions on derived data

This is derived data with a measured error rate. Read this before drawing conclusions near a single field.

Not a prevented-planting eligibility determination. The layer shows where water was detected. It does not decide whether a field was eligible, insured, or prevented from planting.

Appropriate and inappropriate uses

Good uses

  • Seeing where water persisted across a county or region
  • Prioritising fields for follow-up or inspection
  • Supporting evidence alongside field records
  • Comparing cutoffs to judge how long water stood

Don’t use it to

  • Decide prevented-planting eligibility on its own
  • Approve or deny an individual claim
  • Measure exact acreage of a single field
  • Infer conditions outside the planting window

Known limitations

  • Clouds and gaps. Pixels with no clear observation are nodata (255), not dry.
  • Mixed pixels. Edges of small ponds (under ~10 m across) may be missed or blurred at 3 m.
  • Hidden water. Water under dense vegetation or crop canopy can be under-detected.
  • Cutoff meaning. The cutoff is a share of clear observations, not of calendar days.
  • Validation scope. Accuracy was measured in one county (Ramsey, ND). Other counties may differ.

Accuracy assessment

Method. 884 geolocated field photographs from the Ramsey County, ND survey were compared with standing-water detection from the nearest-date imagery.

MetricWhat it tells you
Precision 0.948When the layer says water, it was water in about 95 of 100 cases.
Recall 0.945Of the water seen in photos, the layer found about 95 of 100.
F1 0.946Balance of precision and recall.
Accuracy 0.910Share of all photo points labelled correctly, water or dry.

This assessment is updated as more validation is completed. Changes are logged in Version updates.
